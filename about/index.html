About Chris Abraham

I've lived in the Washington, DC area since 1988 and work from Arlington, Virginia. Gerris Corp is my consultancy, and I'm the person who does the work.

Career

  • Employee number 13 at New Media Strategies, where online reputation management grew up as a practice.
  • Senior account supervisor on Edelman's digital public affairs team in Washington.
  • Co-founder of Abraham Harrison, then Gerris Corp, which I started with Dan Krueger in 2007.
  • Reputation and executive privacy work at Reputation.com.
  • Earlier, work with Howard Rheingold at Rheingold Associates, and internet infrastructure work in the Treasury Department's Office of the CIO.

Technical background

I was a programmer before I was a marketer: Perl, PHP, Python, and C. I've built websites since 1994 and have run chrisabraham.com on Plone, a Python CMS, since the early 2000s. Today I work in Git, on Linux servers, and alongside AI coding tools on my own projects.

Online community

I published the Memetic Lexicon on memes.org in 1999, ran the MemeSpace community, which The New York Times profiled, and founded and moderate r/slowjogging. I was a member of The WELL, eWorld, and ArtsWire.

Writing

I have a BA in American Literature from The George Washington University, with a year at the University of East Anglia in Norwich. I captained my high school speech and debate team, taught creative writing, and write several long essays a week on Substack. I'm also the host of The Chris Abraham Show.
