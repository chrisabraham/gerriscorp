## What I do now

I'm a hands-on technical consultant whose deepest specialty is search. Clients bring me in when the technical side of their web presence needs someone in charge: I diagnose, scope, write the spec, direct the developers, human and AI, ship the parts I can ship myself, and explain it all to leadership in plain English. My technical SEO core covers on-page SEO, site speed, Cloudflare and CDN setup, 301 redirects, indexing, Google Search Console, and migrations. Around it I work as a fractional technical lead, rescue stalled projects, build small AI tools with Claude Code, clean up data, advise founders and executives, and manage the online reputation of people whose public record matters.

## Systems and platforms

I provision and harden my own Linux servers on DigitalOcean: key-only SSH, root and password login disabled, firewalls locked down, and unattended security updates. I run a self-hosted, encrypted file-sync system across my machines, a persistent terminal email setup over SSH, and my own command-line assistant, [Blackbox](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/built-with-claude-code/). I've worked with WordPress for twenty years, including Elementor, Avada, Rank Math, and WooCommerce, and with Shopify and Liquid, Magento 2 and Hyvä, Umbraco, Drupal, Joomla, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, Framer, and React applications, with Cloudflare, GA4, and Search Console throughout. I've also worked under HIPAA and nondisclosure agreements on a healthcare site.

## Technical background

I was a programmer before I was a marketer: Perl, PHP, Python, and C. I've built websites since 1994: a site for my father's company, Pacific Rim Productions, plus urban.net and MemeSpace, and an early run of self-hosted publishing tools, from Greymatter blogs and Movable Type's Perl CGI scripts to PHP-Nuke and PostNuke portals. My first optimization was for my own sites, before Google existed: getting them listed in the web directories of the day, Yahoo and Ask Jeeves among them, and mentioned across Usenet and the early forums. By 1998 that meant on-page work too: keyword targeting, meta tags, titles, and how each page was structured. I have run [chrisabraham.com](https://chrisabraham.com/) on Plone, a Python CMS, since the early 2000s. Today I work in Git, on Linux servers over SSH, in the command line, and alongside AI coding assistants, building my own tools for research, publishing, and task management.
