Every engagement starts with a paid diagnostic: a defined question, the evidence, the cause, and a prioritized plan with an owner for each item. Then I make the changes I have access to, give your developers specs with acceptance criteria for the rest, and check the live result once it ships. Everything is in writing, in Basecamp or by email, with a technical document for the engineers and a plain-English summary for leadership.
