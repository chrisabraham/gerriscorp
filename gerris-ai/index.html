Field notes: how the machines actually read

The working rules behind every Gerris engagement, stated in the open.

Engines are literal

Google and the AI answer engines take your words at face value. If a phrase is not on the page, in the form buyers actually ask it, the page does not come up for it. There is no thesaurus at the other end, and nobody at Google is inferring what you meant.

Being summarized is mandatory

You do not get to opt out of being described by AI answers. You only choose whether the description is assembled from your facts or from your competitor's.

Consistency is the ranking signal

When your website, your Google Business Profile, and your listings disagree, the answer engine goes with the version it can corroborate. One story, told identically everywhere, is what the machines reward.

Frequently asked questions

What is AEO?

AEO is answer engine optimization. It shapes your website and profiles so AI answer engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) describe your business accurately and name you in the answer. Search results used to be a list of links. The top of the page is now a written answer, and AEO is the work of earning your place inside it.

What is GEO?

GEO is generative engine optimization. It makes your business easy for generative AI systems to find, understand, verify, and cite: consistent facts across your site and profiles, entity schema, clear direct answers, and pages the crawlers can read. GEO and AEO rest on the same foundation as SEO.

Yes. AI answers are assembled from crawlable pages, search indexes, business profiles, and directory listings. A site search engines cannot crawl or understand starves the answer engines too. Gerris treats SEO as the foundation and AEO and GEO as the work built on it.
