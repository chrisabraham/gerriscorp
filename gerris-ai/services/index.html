Services: AI visibility first, SEO underneath, builds in the same shop

Gerris sells three things: knowing what the AI answers say about you, fixing the sources those answers are built from, and building the fast static sites and automation the work runs on. Chris Abraham leads every engagement. Specialist delivery runs through trusted partners, named in the open. Prices and commitments are in writing before work begins.

What is the AI Visibility Audit?

$750 flat. We test how your business appears across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini for the searches that bring you revenue. We document what the AI answers say about you, where you are missing, and where a competitor is being named instead. We check your entity and name-address-phone consistency across your site, your Google Business Profile, and your directory listings. AI answers are assembled from those sources; when they disagree, the answer names the business whose facts line up. The deliverable is a written report with every fix in priority order. It is yours to keep regardless of what follows.

What is the Claim Sprint?

The audit tells you what is broken. The sprint fixes the layer that fixes it:

  • Technical foundation: full crawl, Search Console review, titles and metas, broken links and redirects.
  • The claim layer: entity schema with sameAs links, an llms.txt file, a canonical page that states what your business is, and FAQ schema, so the machines can quote you instead of guessing at you.

Where an item belongs with your developers, we return it as a precise written fix list they can execute directly, and we verify it on the live site once they ship it. The sprint is $1,500 flat.

What is AI Presence, Measured?

AI answers drift. Competitors publish, directories change, and the answer that named you in March may name someone else in June. Every month we re-run your money-query set across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity, and you get a written receipt: what changed, what the answers say now, and the next fixes in line. AI Presence is $500 a month, shaped around your search set.

Why does the Google Business Profile matter to AI answers?

For local and service businesses, the Google Business Profile is one of the primary sources AI answers are built from. A suspended, duplicate, or miscategorized profile damages your map listing and corrupts what the AI says about you everywhere else. Gerris's profile work, including service-area configuration and reinstatement support, runs under the same direction as the search work, so your profile, your website, and your listings tell one consistent story. That consistency is what the machines reward.

What SEO does Gerris do?

The foundation, practiced since 1998:

  • Technical SEO audits: every URL crawled, each problem traced to the template, component, or script behind it, fixed at the cause, then crawled again to prove it held.
  • Indexing repair: pages crawled but not indexed, discovered but never crawled, soft 404s, canonical conflicts, misleading sitemaps.
  • Migrations and redirects: full URL inventories, one-to-one 301 maps, launch-day checks, recovery after a move that already lost traffic.
  • Schema and entities: structured data that validates, matches the visible page, and ties your organization, people, and locations together.
  • Speed: Core Web Vitals diagnosis, image and script cleanup, caching, and Cloudflare rules that still welcome crawlers.
  • Measurement setup: Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and GA4, verified and owned by you.

What can AI do inside a business, beyond answering questions?

Plenty, once somebody who knows the work sets it up. Gerris designs and tests the working tools: custom assistants trained on your documents, drafting pipelines that produce first drafts your staff can trust, review checklists that run the same way every time, and prompt sets written for the job instead of borrowed from a listicle. Every tool ships with the instructions, the test set, and a plain account of where it fails. An AI tool nobody has tested against the real work is a demo, and demos do not survive contact with a Tuesday.

What does Gerris build?

The site is static HTML, written and assembled by AI under our direction, deployed on GitHub's hosting, and handed to you outright: your domain, your repository, your files. Can AI agents use your website?

Your customers have started sending software to do their errands. An AI agent opens your site, reads it, fills in your form, and tries to finish the job: a quote request, a booking, a purchase.
