repository on the branch "gerris-ai-alpha1" ONLY. gerris.ai is a new,
independent project; this folder is a build-staging area, not part of

The standalone build (with its CNAME and binary brand assets) lives in
~/workspace/gerris-ai/site/ in the desk workspace, ready to push the
day the chrisabraham/gerris-ai repository exists.
