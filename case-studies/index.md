# Case studies

Each case describes the problem, my role, what I did, who else did what, how the work was checked, and where it stands now. Clients are described by industry; I name clients only with their permission.

## Content the crawler never sees
- [Magento store: product content Googlebot never saw](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/ecommerce-rendering/). Alpine.js sections that mounted only on scroll, and a backlink spike kept on its own track.
- [WordPress media site: a consent banner hiding every article](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/consent-blocking/). Consent blocking kept the article tabs empty for Googlebot.
- [React event platform: 2,000 pages Google couldn't reach](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/react-event-platform/). A 13-page developer spec, ranked P0 to P3.

## Migrations, rescues, and modern stacks
- [Clinical practice: WordPress to Netlify, with search intact](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/netlify-migration/). Redirect decisions for every URL and an AI-assisted repository analysis.
- [Local service business on Sanity and Vercel](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/sanity-vercel/). Metadata changes merged through GitHub, and the developer's prerendering verified.
- [Specialty retailer: cleaning up after Squarespace to Shopify](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/shopify-migration/). 3,400 404s, duplicate URLs, and theme schema.
- [Consumer brand: taking over a stalled React application](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/app-takeover/). An inventory, then fixes on the server over SSH.

## Indexing at scale and recovery
- [Programmatic site: crawling and indexing at scale](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/programmatic-indexing/). Segmented sitemaps and parameter rules for 20,000+ pages.
- [Health-testing store: recovering after an SEO plugin outage](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/indexing-recovery/). Recovery checks in Google and Bing, explained in plain English.

## Entities, locations, and planning
- [Multi-location brand: untangling the parent from its first location](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/multi-location-entity/). Entity structure and developer-ready JSON-LD.
- [Seasonal retailer: a staged plan around the peak](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/seasonal-retailer/). Multi-site audit and timing around the busiest weeks.
- [Therapy practice: a suspended Google Business Profile reinstated](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/gbp-reinstatement/). A documented appeal.

## Content operations and AI search
