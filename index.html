Google Business Profile pricing

If your profile is suspended, missing, or was never set up, you're losing local leads. All work is paid in advance. Google has the final say on timing.

  • Reinstatement: $500 flat. Diagnosis, audit, compliance fixes, and a full appeal. If I can't get your profile reinstated, you get your $500 back. Usually 5 to 14 days.
  • New profile and verification: $500 flat. Set up correctly from day one. Usually 3 to 7 days.
  • Optimization and protection: $500 flat. Categories, services, description, photos, NAP consistency, and defense against spam and fake reviews. 7 to 10 days.
  • Ongoing management: from $350 a month. Weekly posts, review responses, spam cleanup, and reporting. The growth plan, at $750 a month, adds review campaigns and quarterly optimization. Cancel with 30 days' notice.
  • Fast-track consultation: $250. A 30-minute strategy call, credited toward any later service.

Complex or multi-location cases may be quoted higher after a first look. I never fabricate evidence or misstate facts to Google.

What does Gerris Corp do?

Usually. Reinstatement is a $500 flat fee covering diagnosis, audit, compliance fixes, and a full appeal to Google, typically 5 to 14 days. If the profile is not reinstated, the $500 is refunded.

Do you buy links?

No. Gerris Corp does organic search engine optimization only: better, faster, more complete sites with better content. No black-hat link building and no paid ads.

No one honestly can. The goal is a large, lasting improvement in visibility. Most sites see significant improvement in 3 to 6 months, depending on the competition and the site's starting point.
