I help businesses get found and fix what's holding their websites back

I'm Chris Abraham, and Gerris is my consultancy. I work with funded companies, brands, and high-profile people. When a site goes quiet in search, I find the mechanism, explain it in plain language, and work with your people until the fix is live and checked.

What I do

  • Technical SEO forensics and fixes: traffic drops, indexing problems, rendering, redirects, and structured data, diagnosed with evidence.
  • Website migrations: redirect maps and launch checks before the move, and cleanup after it.
  • AI search and content: SEO, AEO, and GEO, so search engines and AI assistants understand and represent you accurately.
  • Working with your developers: search problems turned into tickets with a mechanism, examples, and acceptance criteria.

See every service, including ongoing support, reputation work, Google Business Profile hard cases, and email and DNS cleanup.

How I work

Every engagement starts with a paid diagnostic: a defined question, the evidence, the cause, and a prioritized set of fixes with an owner for each. Then I make the changes I have access to, hand your developers clear tickets for the rest, and verify the result once it ships. Details are on Work With Me.

Recent work

  • Traced an e-commerce traffic loss to product templates that rendered specs, FAQs, and reviews only on scroll, which crawlers never saw.
  • Reconciled URLs and redirects for a WordPress to Netlify migration.
  • Moved hillmole.com, my serialized novel of 309 entries, from Movable Type to static pages on GitHub Pages with every original URL intact.
