Website migrations

A migration is the moment a site is most likely to lose what it built in search. I help before the move, during the launch, and after it.

Before launch

  • An inventory of every URL that matters: pages, media, PDFs, and anything with links or traffic.
  • A redirect map from every old URL to its best new home, with deliberate decisions about pages you're retiring.
  • A review of the new templates for titles, canonicals, structured data, and content that crawlers can read.

At launch

  • Redirects tested in bulk on the live site.
  • Sitemaps, robots rules, and Search Console updated.
  • A quick round of fixes with your developer or host.

After launch

Something already went wrong? I reconcile old and new URLs, find orphaned pages and broken media, fix redirect gaps, and track down what changed. I'm useful when the marketing team, the developer, the host, and the owner each hold part of the answer.

Platforms

WordPress, Umbraco, Wix, Shopify, Magento, Netlify, GitHub Pages, and static sites, including a WordPress to Netlify migration and a Movable Type to GitHub Pages move.
