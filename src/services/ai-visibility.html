Why this, why now

Search results used to be ten links and a choice. Today the top of the page is a written answer, produced by AI systems that assemble it from your website, your Google Business Profile, your directory listings, and whatever else they can verify about you. When those sources disagree, the AI goes with the version it can corroborate, which is often the competitor whose facts line up.

Review searches, the "best" and "which one" queries buyers type, moved furthest in that tracking. The answer on top is now the first impression, the shortlist, and often the decision.
