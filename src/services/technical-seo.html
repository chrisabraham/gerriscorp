Technical SEO forensics and fixes

A site lost traffic and nobody knows why. My job is to find the mechanism, prove it, and get it fixed.

When to call me

  • Organic traffic fell after a redesign, a platform change, a template update, or for no visible reason.
  • Search Console reports pages as "Crawled, currently not indexed" or "Discovered, currently not indexed."
  • Important pages rank below thin ones, or the wrong URL ranks.
  • The site looks complete to people and empty to crawlers.

What I check

  • Rendering: what crawlers actually receive, especially on JavaScript-heavy builds using Alpine, React, Next.js, or Vue.
  • Crawling and indexing: robots rules, sitemaps, canonical tags, status codes, and duplicate URLs.
  • Redirects and broken links, including chains, loops, and redirects to the wrong place.
  • Internal linking, titles, descriptions, and heading structure.
  • Structured data and how it matches the visible page.
  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals.
  • Backlink anomalies, such as a sudden spike in spam links.

One cause at a time

Two problems often arrive together: a template change and a spam link spike, or a redirect error and a seasonal dip. I keep each on its own track until the data connects them, so every fix targets a proven cause.

What you get

  • Findings with evidence: crawl data, Search Console reports, and example URLs.
  • The mechanism, explained in plain language for owners and in technical detail for developers.
  • A prioritized list of fixes, each with an owner.
  • Developer tickets with affected URLs, observed behavior, and acceptance criteria.
  • A check of the live site once the fixes ship.

I make the changes I have access to myself, in the CMS or the repository.

Tools

Google Search Console, GA4, Screaming Frog, Semrush, Ahrefs, PageSpeed Insights, Cloudflare, and the browser's own developer tools.
