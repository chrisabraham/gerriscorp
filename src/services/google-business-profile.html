Google Business Profile hard cases

Suspensions, failed video verifications, profiles that are live in public and stuck in the dashboard, and access problems across managed profiles.

A Google Business Profile is often the first thing a local customer sees, and when it's suspended or stuck, calls and visits drop at once. Google handles these cases through automated systems, an appeals process, and a support queue that moves at its own pace. The cases I take are the hard ones: repeated suspensions, verification loops, duplicate or split profiles, and accounts where one problem has spread to every profile they manage.

Advisory work, priced for the effort

I take on Google Business Profile cases as paid advisory engagements or as part of an ongoing SEO or reputation retainer. Each stage is scoped and paid in advance, so the work is defined before it starts.

Stage one: a written diagnostic

A prepaid review of the profile, its history, the account and its other profiles, the business's documentation, and Google's guidelines, delivered as a written findings report:

  • What most likely triggered the problem, based on the evidence available.
  • What documentation exists and what's missing.
  • Whether an appeal or reverification is viable, and what it would involve.
  • What to fix first, whether or not you hire me for the next stage.

Stage two: remediation and appeal

When there's a viable path, a separately scoped engagement covers compliance fixes to the profile and website, the appeal, and correspondence with Google, with agreed limits on profiles, appeal rounds, and hours. I build appeals like a legal brief: the strongest verifiable document first, nothing that exposes private information, and only claims the paperwork supports. Further rounds are agreed and paid separately.

What I need from you

Truthful documentation, access to the profile and account, and prompt answers to Google's requests. Google makes every reinstatement decision, on its own schedule.

Prevention

Most suspensions trace back to a mismatch between the profile and reality: an address that's really a service area, a name with extra keywords, a category that doesn't fit, or information that differs from the website. Fixing those before Google notices is far cheaper than an appeal, and it fits naturally into ongoing support.

Local search beyond the profile

A profile is only as strong as the website and listings behind it. Consistent name, address, and phone details across directories, location and service pages that match the profile, and reviews answered promptly all support local visibility.

Common questions

How long does reinstatement take?

Google's appeal reviews take anywhere from days to several weeks, and complex cases take longer. The diagnostic itself is delivered within a week of receiving access and documents.

Do you manage profiles that aren't suspended?

Yes, as part of local SEO within ongoing support or a broader SEO engagement.
