Case studies

Each case describes the problem, my role, what I did, who else did what, how the work was checked, and where it stands now. Clients are described by industry; I name clients only with their permission.

Content the crawler never sees

  • Magento store: product content Googlebot never saw. Alpine.js sections that mounted only on scroll, and a backlink spike kept on its own track.
  • WordPress media site: a consent banner hiding every article. Consent blocking kept the article tabs empty for Googlebot.
  • React event platform: 2,000 pages Google couldn't reach. A 13-page developer spec, ranked P0 to P3.

Migrations, rescues, and modern stacks

  • Clinical practice: WordPress to Netlify, with search intact. Redirect decisions for every URL and an AI-assisted repository analysis.
  • Local service business on Sanity and Vercel. Metadata changes merged through GitHub, and the developer's prerendering verified.
  • Specialty retailer: cleaning up after Squarespace to Shopify. 3,400 404s, duplicate URLs, and theme schema.
  • Consumer brand: taking over a stalled React application. An inventory, then fixes on the server over SSH.

Indexing at scale and recovery

  • Programmatic site: crawling and indexing at scale. Segmented sitemaps and parameter rules for 20,000+ pages.
  • Health-testing store: recovering after an SEO plugin outage. Recovery checks in Google and Bing, explained in plain English.

Entities, locations, and planning

  • Multi-location brand: untangling the parent from its first location. Entity structure and developer-ready JSON-LD.
  • Seasonal retailer: a staged plan around the peak. Multi-site audit and timing around the busiest weeks.
  • Therapy practice: a suspended Google Business Profile reinstated. A documented appeal.

Content operations and AI search

  • Two B2B companies on Umbraco. 54 landing pages, steady publishing, and cache and template fixes.
  • AI company: search visibility for recruiting engineers. How AI answers represent an employer.

Data

  • Contact data: three cleanups and an AI audit. 88,900 contacts, two merged exports, and a tracker that invented its data.

My own builds

  • Built with Claude Code. Blackbox, my command-line assistant with 106 tests; Hill Mole's move off Movable Type; and this site.
