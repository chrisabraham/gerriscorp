Privacy

This site collects very little: anonymous visit statistics, and whatever you choose to send me by email or phone.

Visit statistics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits, see which pages people read, and learn how visitors arrive, including from search engines and AI assistants. It records information such as the pages you view, your approximate location, your device and browser, and the site that referred you. It doesn't collect your name, email address, or other details that identify you directly.

For visitors in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, analytics cookies are switched off. Google Analytics receives cookieless measurements instead, which count visits without storing anything on your device. Elsewhere, Google Analytics may set first-party cookies to recognize repeat visits. Advertising and personalization features are turned off for everyone.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can block Google Analytics anywhere with your browser's privacy settings, a content blocker, or Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Email and phone

When you email me or call, I receive what you send: your name, your address or number, and the details of your project. I use it only to reply and to do the work you hire me for. I don't sell it, share it with marketers, or add you to a mailing list.

Hosting and security

The site is static pages served by GitHub Pages, with DNS through Cloudflare. Those services keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and reliability, under their own policies. There are no forms, accounts, or comments on this site.

Client information

Information I see while working for clients, such as analytics, Search Console data, site access, and documents, is kept confidential, used only for the engagement, and covered by a nondisclosure agreement where the client asks for one.
