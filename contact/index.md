## What happens next
1. I read what you sent and answer it, most often by the next business day.
2. If I'm the right person, a short call or a few emails to understand the problem.
3. A written scope for a paid diagnostic: the questions, the deliverable, the timeline, and the price.
4. Work starts once the scope is agreed and the diagnostic is paid.

## Useful to include
- The URL, plus the CMS or framework behind it, if you know which one.
- The date the trouble began and what else happened that week: a redesign, a migration, a plugin update, a hosting move.
- Screenshots from Search Console or analytics, if you have them.
- Who else is involved: a developer, an agency, a host.

## Good reasons to get in touch
- Organic traffic fell and nobody can explain it.
- Your website, developer, and vendors need someone in charge.
- A migration or redesign is coming, or one just went wrong.
- You inherited a project and don't know what you have.
- You'd like a small AI tool built safely, or a second opinion on someone else's plan.
- Email from your domain is landing in spam.
