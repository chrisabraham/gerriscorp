# B2B catalog SEO for manufacturers and distributors

Your best customer may be searching for a specific component, a material, or a compatibility answer. I help suppliers make that information findable and useful, then connect the page to a quote request your sales team can act on.

## When this service fits

This is for businesses whose catalog supports a considered purchase: industrial components, technical equipment, specialist materials, or products sold through quotations and repeat accounts. It is especially useful when a site has thousands of items but weak specifications, inaccessible product information, or traffic that rarely becomes a suitable inquiry.

A retail checkout and a supplier qualification process need different content. I review how your customers buy before recommending more pages. Sales conversations, product documentation, Search Console, and a sample crawl establish whether the immediate problem is discovery, missing information, or the route into sales.

## What the diagnostic covers
- Product family and part number discovery, including important identifiers and variant distinctions.
- Specifications, datasheets, selection guidance, and the information buyers need before contacting sales.
- HTML rendering, internal navigation, duplicate URLs, filters, and canonical consistency.
- Product markup checked against the visible information and applicable search requirements.
- Quote forms, product context, confirmation behavior, and the destination of test inquiries.
- A measurement plan that distinguishes total submissions from commercially relevant opportunities.

## A pilot before a catalog rollout

I choose one product family with your commercial and technical reviewers. The first delivery is a written findings list, a page inventory, and a proposed template with acceptance criteria. Each recommendation identifies its evidence, its owner, and what must be checked before it can be published.

Implementation can include editing priority pages, preparing developer specifications, or working in the repository where access permits. I verify the approved changes against the original sample. A pilot report records what shipped, what still depends on your team, and what the early observations do and do not establish.

## What I need from you

Access to the relevant site data, a representative catalog export, approved documentation, and someone who can verify technical claims. Sales needs to define a useful inquiry and identify recurring customer questions. I can organize and explain your source material; an authorized product expert must approve specifications, certifications, and replacement recommendations.

Read the [technical buyer catalog guide](https://gerriscorp.com/guides/b2b-catalog-seo/) for the selection and measurement method. For a related implementation problem, the [ecommerce rendering case study](https://gerriscorp.com/case-studies/ecommerce-rendering/) explains how product content can be present visually yet difficult for a crawler to receive. It is evidence of that technical issue, not a forecast of your commercial results.

## Questions before starting

### Does every SKU need a rewritten description?

No. I first identify which pages need distinct information and which problems come from shared templates. A focused product family establishes a standard before a larger editorial commitment.

### Can a quote-only catalog use this service?

Yes. The goal is to help a buyer qualify the product and contact the right person. I do not add fictional prices or promise shopping features for pages that do not meet their requirements.
