Ongoing SEO and website support

A continuing pair of experienced hands for businesses that need steady improvement and someone who already knows the site.

What it covers

  • Technical SEO fixes and monitoring in Search Console.
  • Writing, editing, and publishing pages in your CMS, with titles, descriptions, internal links, and structured data.
  • Troubleshooting when something breaks or traffic moves.
  • Coordinating with your developers, host, and marketing team.
  • A short monthly report on what changed and what's next.

How it works

You and I agree on a monthly capacity and a priority list. The retainer is billed at the start of each month, and work is tracked in Basecamp or by email so you always know the status.
