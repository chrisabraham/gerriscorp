# Ongoing SEO and website support

A continuing pair of experienced hands for businesses that need steady improvement and someone who already knows the site.

Websites drift. Plugins update, pages get added in a hurry, a developer changes a template, a redirect breaks, Google changes how it treats something. Ongoing support means somebody is watching, fixing, and improving every month, without the overhead of a large agency retainer.

## What it covers
- Technical SEO fixes and Search Console monitoring, with problems caught before they cost traffic.
- Writing, editing, and publishing pages in your CMS, with titles, descriptions, internal links, and structured data.
- Site speed and Cloudflare upkeep.
- AI search checks: a recurring look at what the major assistants say about you.
- Troubleshooting when something breaks or traffic moves.
- Coordinating with your developers, host, and marketing team.
- A short monthly report: what changed, what I fixed, what's next, and what needs your decision.

## How it works

You and I agree on a monthly capacity and a priority list. The retainer is billed at the start of each month. Work is tracked in Basecamp or by email, so you always know the status, and priorities can change month to month as the business does.

## Who it suits

Businesses with a site that matters to revenue and no full-time SEO on staff, marketing teams that need a specialist they can call on, and agencies that need experienced technical SEO help for their own clients.

## A typical month

A check of Search Console and analytics for anything new or broken. Two or three pages written, improved, or republished with proper metadata. A round of technical fixes from the priority list. A look at site speed after the latest plugin or theme updates. Answers to your developer's questions. Then a short written report, and an updated list for next month.

## What makes it work

A clear priority list that you own, access to the systems I need, and a single written channel for requests, so nothing gets lost in text messages. The best ongoing relationships feel like having an experienced technical person on staff for a fraction of the cost.

## Combining services

Ongoing support pairs naturally with the rest of my work. It's the follow-through after a [technical SEO diagnosis](https://gerriscorp.com/services/technical-seo/), the monitoring after a [migration](https://gerriscorp.com/services/migrations/), and the steady implementation behind a [fractional technical lead](https://gerriscorp.com/services/technical-lead/) arrangement.

## Common questions

### Is there a minimum term?

Ongoing support runs month to month after an initial diagnostic, so the first month starts from a clear picture of the site.

### What happens to unused hours?

The capacity is reserved for you each month. Whether unused time carries over is set out in the written agreement before work starts.
