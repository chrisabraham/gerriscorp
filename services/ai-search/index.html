AI search and content

People now ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews before they ever click a link. I help those systems, and search engines, understand and represent your business accurately.

SEO, AEO, and GEO

SEO is search engine optimization. AEO, answer engine optimization, shapes content so it answers the questions people actually ask. GEO, generative engine optimization, makes your business easy for AI systems to find, understand, and cite. All three rest on the same foundation: clear pages, useful answers, coherent structure, accurate structured data, and a site crawlers can read.

AI search readiness audit

I test a set of real prompts across the major AI assistants and record what they say about you, your products, and your competitors, where those answers come from, and what's missing or wrong. You get the findings and a prioritized plan.

Content that answers the question

I write and improve service pages, landing pages, FAQs, glossaries, titles, and descriptions, and I publish them properly in your CMS. A service page plainly explains what you do, how it helps, and why your approach makes sense. An article answers its question completely, with the context a reader or an AI needs to quote it accurately.

Machine-readable you

For founders, experts, and brands: consistent facts across your site and profiles, schema.org structured data that ties them together, and a self-contained body of pages that AI systems can cite. I've practiced this on myself for two decades by publishing my own work in the open.

Readable without JavaScript

Many AI crawlers read only the HTML your server sends. I check that every important word is in it.

This site follows its own advice: static HTML, schema.org data on every page, and an llms.txt summary for language models.
