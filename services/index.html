Services

I'm the person you call when the technical side of your web presence needs someone in charge. Every engagement starts with a paid diagnostic and a clear next step, and I do the work myself, alongside your team.

Consulting and technical direction

  • AI workflow testing: acceptance cases for a real pilot, with expected outputs, permission boundaries, failure evidence, and a clear next decision.
  • Fractional technical lead: one experienced person who owns your web stack part time, directs your developers and vendors, ships fixes, and reports to leadership in plain English.
  • Developer specs and implementation QA: a diagnosis turned into a prioritized spec with evidence and acceptance criteria, then verification that the fix actually shipped.
  • Agency partner and white-label support: technical SEO, AI search, and developer coordination inside your agency's client relationships.
  • AI agents and automation, built safely: small AI tools built with OpenAI Codex and Claude Code, with human confirmation on every action, tests, and encrypted backups.
  • Project rescue, takeovers, and legacy migrations: stalled or inherited projects inventoried, stabilized, and moved off dead software.
  • Data cleanup and AI-output audits: contact lists, CRMs, and AI-generated spreadsheets verified, deduplicated, and rebuilt.
  • Analytics and measurement: GA4 and Search Console you can trust, and an honest reading of what the numbers prove.
  • Strategic advisory and second opinions: advice for founders and executives on AI, search, reputation, and technology decisions.

Search and site performance

  • Internal linking strategy: connect services, articles, products, and case studies with contextual links and a practical implementation map.
  • Technical SEO forensics: traffic drops and indexing problems diagnosed with evidence and fixed with your developers.
  • SEO engineering for Next.js, React, and headless sites: titles, canonicals, sitemaps, redirects, and schema written as code, tested on every build, and checked on a preview before merge.
  • Content the crawler never sees: my signature diagnosis, for pages that look complete to visitors and empty to Google and AI crawlers.
  • Large and programmatic sites: crawling and indexing managed across tens of thousands of pages.
  • Ecommerce SEO: Shopify, Magento, and WooCommerce fixed at the template level.
  • On-page SEO: titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, and structured data, written and published.
  • Site speed, Core Web Vitals, and Cloudflare: fast pages on real phones, and a CDN configured properly.
  • WordPress care: caching, hardening, or a static move: Cloudflare page caching, hardening for what the cache can't shield, or a move off WordPress.
  • Google Search Console: setup, cleanup, and fixes for what the indexing reports reveal.
  • Website migrations: URL inventories, 301 redirect maps, launch checks, and cleanup.
  • AI search visibility: SEO, AEO, and GEO, so ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI features represent you accurately.
  • Fact-checked writing in your voice: articles and pages written with AI from your own material, then checked claim by claim before they go live.
  • Podcast websites, built and run for you: a page and transcript for every episode, generated from your feed and updated every morning.
  • Ongoing SEO and website support: an agreed amount of experienced help each month.

Local, reputation, and email

  • Multi-location and connected businesses: entity structure, location pages, and sister brands kept distinct.
  • Reputation and perception management: for founders, executives, and families who need privacy and an accurate public record.
  • Google Business Profile hard cases: suspensions and verification loops, starting with a written diagnostic.
  • Email authentication and DNS: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, deliverability, and domain consolidation.
